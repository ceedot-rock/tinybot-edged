# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

- Email: corey@slidphilabs.com with the subject line `tinybot-edged security`
- Or use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository
  (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability")

Include the affected file, steps or inputs to reproduce, and what you expected
versus what happened.

You can expect an acknowledgement within 3 business days. We will keep you
updated while we investigate and credit you in the changelog unless you prefer
to stay anonymous.

## In scope

- Anything that lets the 3x3 frame deviate from its documented build behavior
- Build or tooling issues that could silently corrupt frame output
- Private keys, tokens, or credentials accidentally committed to the repo

## Out of scope

- Deployments we do not run
- Social engineering, spam, or denial-of-service against anything hosted
